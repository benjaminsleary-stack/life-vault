## Sat 8 Aug — Wimpole Estate: Summer of Play (Pokémon Trail)
National Trust · ~25 min drive · adult £18 / child £9 / family £45, free for NT members · [events](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/wimpole-estate/events)
Pokémon-themed play trail, giant chess and craft/story days running daily to 2
Sep; heavy horse carriage rides also on that weekend. Toddler- and
buggy-friendly grounds.

## Sat 15 Aug — Anglesey Abbey: Summer of Play
National Trust · ~20 min drive · adult £18 (£10 off-peak after 2pm), free for NT members · [events](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/anglesey-abbey-gardens-and-lode-mill/events)

31 Aug, plus Pokémon zones. Good pushchair access.

## Sat 22 Aug — Audley End House and Gardens: Great British Summer
English Heritage · ~35 min drive · included with general admission (price not

grounds, Fridays–Sundays to 31 Aug.
