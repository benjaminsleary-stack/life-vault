## Fri 20 Nov — Wimpole Estate: Christmas at Wimpole
National Trust · ~25 min drive · opening night · adult off-peak £18 / standard £26, child off-peak £13 / peak £19.50, £1 for under-3s, free for NT members (book ahead), parking £8 · [book](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/wimpole-estate/christmas-at-wimpole)
Runs 20 Nov–3 Jan. Illuminated trail through the gardens — new Ribbon Tunnel,
Christmas Cathedral, Fire Garden. Timed entry, limited capacity; an early
off-peak slot keeps it well clear of Milo's bedtime. Pushchair-suitable paths.

## Sun 22 Nov — Audley End: Enchanted Railway Christmas Special
Privately run (Audley End estate, English Heritage site) · ~40 min drive ·
adult/child over 92cm £23.50–£40.50, child under 92cm £11.75–£20.25 ·
[book](https://tickets.audley-end-railway.co.uk/event-tickets/77310?catID=72506)
Runs 21 Nov–24 Dec, daytime (site opens 9am–5pm). Train ride through an
illuminated woodland, Father Christmas and Mother Christmas greet each
carriage, reindeer food and a Christmas letter along the way. Pram-friendly,
suits both ages — this is a longer day out, allow 3–4 hours on site.

## Sat 28 Nov — Cambridge Botanic Lights
Cambridge University Botanic Garden · in Cambridge, no real drive ·
adult off-peak £18.15 / standard £24.75, child (4–15) £13.50–£17, under-4s
free · [book](https://www.cambridgelivetickets.co.uk/events/cambridge-botanic-lights-2026)
Runs 27 Nov–23 Dec, 4:30–8:30pm. Light trail through the Garden after dark —
flat paths, buggy-accessible. Sits after the Saturday morning class with
plenty of day left, and an early evening slot still clears Milo's bedtime.

## Sun 13 Dec — Anglesey Abbey: Meet Father Christmas
National Trust · ~15 min drive · £24.95 per child (includes one adult),
normal estate admission on top · [book](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/anglesey-abbey-gardens-and-lode-mill/events/da0d12fe-8e23-4aba-aeaf-4be47731ceb5)
Slots 9:45am–3pm, 5 & 6 Dec, 12 & 13 Dec, and 18–22 Dec — picked the 13th as
the free Sunday. ~30-minute storytelling session before meeting Father
Christmas; National Trust lists it for ages 3–10, so it's a clean fit for
Jasper and a stretch for Milo, but doable with him on a lap.
