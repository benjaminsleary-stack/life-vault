# Family days out — October 2026

For [[Jasper]] (5) and [[Milo]] (2). Checked against the family, personal and
work calendars through 2026-11-14 — all three sources loaded OK. Saturdays are
tied up 9am–12pm by the recurring "Les petits caméléons" class through this
whole window, so the picks below sit on free Sundays instead; 17 Oct is also
out (Rowan's birthday party at 1pm).

## Sun 4 Oct — Wimpole Estate: Halloween in the House + Very Hungry Caterpillar trail
National Trust · ~25 min drive · Whole Estate ticket: adult £21 / child £10.50 / family £52.50, free for NT members · [Halloween in the House](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/wimpole-estate/events/bb855608-d183-4dc9-9974-afad7768cafb)
Runs 1 Oct–1 Nov. Halloween trail through the Hall, 12–3:30pm, plus the Very
Hungry Caterpillar trail outdoors 9:30am–4pm the same dates — a two-for-one
visit. Buggy-friendly grounds, suits both ages.

## Sun 11 Oct — Anglesey Abbey: Three Cheers for Pooh! garden trail & Winnie-the-Pooh exhibition
National Trust · ~20 min drive · adult £19 / child £9.50 / family £47.50, free for NT members · [what's on](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/anglesey-abbey-gardens-and-lode-mill/autumn-at-anglesey-abbey)
Runs 3 Oct–3 Jan (closed 2–15 Nov, so this weekend is well clear of that).
Garden story trail spotting Pooh, Tigger and friends through the grounds —
flat paths, buggy-accessible pace — plus the centenary exhibition in the house.

## Sun 18 Oct — Wicken Fen: Fairy Doors and Folklore Family Trail
National Trust · ~25 min drive · adult £11 / child £5.50 / family £27.50 (plus £3.20 parking), free for NT members · [Wicken Fen](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/wicken-fen-national-nature-reserve)
First day of the trail (runs to 9 Nov). Self-guided stroll round the
Woodland Walk spotting fairy doors — flat boardwalk, pushchair-friendly,
no booking needed.

## Sun 25 Oct — Audley End: Halloween Half-Term "Great Pumpkin Party"
English Heritage · ~40 min drive · standard admission (price not published online — check at booking), free for EH members · [event page](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/whats-on/audley-end-halloween-2026/)
24 Oct–1 Nov, 10am–4pm. Themed trail, interactive storytelling, spooky games
and a daily fancy-dress competition across the grounds — the biggest outing
on this list, and the extra drive time is the trade-off.
