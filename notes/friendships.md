## What to know

An observation from the 1 Aug interview, recorded because it is the kind of thing
that is invisible until someone writes it down.

Asked which of the people in `people/` are friends rather than family, Ben's answer
was **"these are family"** — every one of them. The only two names he could offer
for social contact were [[Joe]] (a colleague) and [[Katie]] (an old colleague he
and [[Charlotte]] see together). Asked when he had last seen someone socially
without the children, the answer was **"no idea"**.

That is the whole social picture the vault currently holds: two people, neither
seen recently enough to date.

**How to use this.** It is context, not a stick. It is directly relevant to
[[mental-health]] — he is in weekly therapy for anxiety and depression, and social
isolation is not a neutral fact next to that. But he did not raise it as a problem
and has not asked for help with it, so it is not a nudge and not a task. If a
genuine, specific opportunity appears — a gig he'd want, a person mentioned in
passing — it is fair to connect it. Generic "you should see friends more" is not.
