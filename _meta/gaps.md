# Gaps — what the vault doesn't know

_One question a day, asked by `evening-brief`, answered by a one-tap reply.
Maintained by `refresh-summaries` (weekly) and `harvest`; crossed off by
`file-inbox` when a capture answers one._

The evening brief used to end with **"Anything to log?"** — an open prompt,
which yielded nine captures in seventeen days. Open questions get scrolled past;
specific ones get answered. This is the list of specific ones.

## How it works

- The evening brief takes the **oldest unasked open gap**, asks it as one line,
  and stamps it `asked: <date>`.
- A gap is asked at most once every **14 days**, and at most **three times**
  total. After that it moves to `## Parked` — the same anti-nag logic as tasks
  (CLAUDE.md rule 3). A question Ben has declined three times is a question he
  is not going to answer, and asking a fourth time is nagging.
- When an answer arrives, `file-inbox` files it normally **and** moves the gap
  to `## Answered` with the date. Nothing is deleted.
- Never ask a `## Private` question here. Anything under a private heading is
  out of scope for a prompt (CLAUDE.md, private sections).
